## Deployment/integration handoff

- GET/POST `/api/cron/reminders` requires `Authorization: Bearer {CRON_SECRET}`; secret must be at least 32 characters. Never expose it in the browser.
- Configure an external scheduled invocation; there is no permanent Next.js timer. Invoke throughout the day for visits and at least daily for IST rent due days. Follow `properties.nextCursor` with `?propertyCursor=...` when present, and rerun while `limited` indicates remaining eligible deliveries.
- Rent reminders clamp due days 29–31 to the month end. They include the supplied rent amount. Expiry notices use 90/60-day windows and notify tenant and owner. No automatic rent escalation, tax calculation, or financial settlement occurs.
- Deliveries use persisted leases, frozen provider payloads, stable idempotency keys, and explicit `needsReconciliation` after 23 hours of ambiguity. Add monitoring for failed/reconciliation/limited results.
- Mobile/SMS/WhatsApp/push integrations do not exist. Display email support only until a provider, consent rules and credentials are supplied.
- Local environment lacks RESEND_API_KEY, FROM_EMAIL, CRON_SECRET, and R2 account/access/secret/bucket variables. Firebase Auth/Data variable names are present; credentials and live IAM were not validated by isolated tests.
- Existing GCS/R2 public URLs can remain accessible to anyone holding a URL. API metadata authorization does not revoke historical public links; a future private signed-download policy needs explicit client/storage approval.
- Resolve the duplicate page routes before rerunning production build and browser acceptance. The old protected guest-routing tests may need the intended public property/search behavior updated.
